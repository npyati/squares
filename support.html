Help & Support

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email squares@nickpyati.com. A note about your device and iOS version helps.

How do I play?

Tap the squares that solve the board. Each colour follows one simple rule and acts on the squares around it — green counts toward the solution, red against it, and the rest bend, silence, extend, and flip from there. The in-app walkthrough (menu → how to play) covers every colour, one at a time.

Is it free? Are there ads?

squares is free. No ads, no tracking, no accounts — nothing between you and the puzzle.

My history or streak isn't syncing between devices

Turn on iCloud sync in settings, make sure the device is signed into iCloud, and relaunch the app. Sync starts on the next launch, and your progress merges across your devices from there.

How do I create and share my own board?

Open create from the menu. Place colours, and the solver checks your board in real time. When it's valid, share the link — anyone can open it in the app or play it in a browser.
